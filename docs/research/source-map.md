The load-bearing method retained for evaluation is isolated divergence followed by a separate critic. Scoville-specific additions under test are bounded prior-art reconciliation, calibrated originality labels, mechanism operators, host-capacity waves with prompts frozen in advance, read-only suite ownership, and an explicit decision stop.

## Research

| Source | Evidence used | Design consequence | Applicability limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tree of Thoughts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601v2) | Multiple reasoning paths, evaluation, lookahead, and backtracking can improve search tasks over linear reasoning. | Preserve multiple candidate paths and a separate selection phase. | The reported tasks are Game of 24, creative writing, and crosswords, not open engineering ideation. |
| [Brainstorm then Select](https://openreview.net/forum?id=8HwKaJ1wvl) | On the Alternate Uses Task, a generation phase followed by selection outperformed brainstorming alone. | Separate divergence from convergence. | A single creativity test does not validate software architecture or multi-agent isolation. |
| [LLM Discussion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06373) | Multi-LLM discussion and role-play were evaluated on several creativity tests. | Include multi-agent methods as a baseline and test search-operator choices. | Creativity-test gains do not prove correctness, usefulness, or safe project decisions. |
| [Creativity in LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1403/) | The survey covers divergent exploration, iterative refinement, collaborative synthesis, and evaluation while identifying inconsistent standards, bias, coordination conflict, and missing unified benchmarks. | Freeze an explicit benchmark, separate exact gates from judgments, and measure coordination and cost. | This is a survey and taxonomy, not direct evidence that one Scoville workflow is superior. |
| [No Evidence for LLMs Being Useful in Problem Reframing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01631) | A 280-participant study found no quality gain from free-form, direct, or structured LLM reframing and found a wider expertise gap. | Keep human selection, negative controls, and practical usefulness as hard evaluation concerns. | Human designer reframing differs from isolated agent-to-agent ideation. |
| [Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces collective diversity](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290) | AI ideas improved individual story evaluations while AI-assisted stories became more similar to each other. | Measure semantic mechanism diversity, not only individual idea scores. | Short-story co-creation is not software or product design. |
