# Add the explicit Research composition protocol

## Goal

Qualify a Brainstorm revision that accepts one Research-owned landscape lane in explicit combined mode without duplicating prior-art work, exposing landscape evidence before convergence, weakening retrieved-content safety, or creating a standalone dependency.

## Non-goals

- Do not merge Research and Brainstorm or activate either sibling automatically.
- Do not replace Brainstorm's native landscape agent in standalone runs.
- Do not let Research own divergent generation, convergence, originality labels, or the shortlist.
- Do not add Research ledgers, provider code, network requirements, or a shared runtime to Brainstorm.
- Do not publish, tag, release, or replace installed bytes before exact qualification evidence and separate publication authorization.
