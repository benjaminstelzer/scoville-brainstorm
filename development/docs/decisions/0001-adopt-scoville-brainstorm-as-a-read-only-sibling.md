# Adopt Scoville Brainstorm as a read-only Scoville sibling

## Decision

Use `Scoville Brainstorm` as the product title and `scoville-brainstorm` as the project and Skill identity. Give it ownership of deliberate pre-decision divergence, bounded prior-art comparison, and compact option synthesis. Keep brainstorming read-only and stop before implementation or durable project planning.

## Problem

The current Scoville siblings optimize reliable implementation, interface quality, wording fidelity, durable planning, and task transfer. None owns the opposite pre-decision need: independently exploring materially different mechanisms and separating established approaches from adaptations, recombinations, and candidate-original directions before choosing what to build.

## Drivers

- Preserve one recognizable Scoville identity and non-overlapping sibling ownership.
- Encourage genuine mechanism diversity without weakening factual, authority, or safety boundaries.
- Prevent ordinary coding work from becoming an expensive multi-agent exercise.
- Keep a clear human decision point before Code or Plan assumes ownership.
- Support bounded research without overstating novelty.

## Considered alternatives

- Add brainstorming to Scoville Code: reuses an existing Skill but conflicts with Code's goal-first, smallest-coherent-solution optimization and broadens its activation surface.
- Use upstream ADHD unchanged: provides isolated branching and criticism but does not own Scoville composition or evidence-based prior-art classification.
- Create Scoville Inspire: offers broader branding but is less precise and risks activation overlap with Scribe and general creative writing.
- Create Scoville Brainstorm as a sibling: gives divergence a narrow, inspectable owner and preserves the later Code and Plan transition.

## Consequences

The repository root and future installable package use `scoville-brainstorm`. Clear English or German requests for multiple unusual solution directions may activate it; ordinary implementation, review, known-answer debugging, and prose requests do not. Brainstorm output may recommend experiments but never executes them under this Skill alone. Research findings remain bounded evidence, and originality claims use qualified categories rather than assertions of objective novelty.

## Confirmation

Verify the final metadata, activation cases, output contract, sibling-composition tests, and installed catalogs: one Scoville Brainstorm identity appears, positive ideation requests activate it, ordinary coding and wording cases do not, and every result stops at a human-selectable shortlist before implementation.

## Revisit when

Another Scoville sibling acquires explicit ownership of divergent ideation, supported hosts provide a first-class brainstorming primitive with equivalent evidence boundaries, or observed activation overlap cannot be corrected without changing the identity.
